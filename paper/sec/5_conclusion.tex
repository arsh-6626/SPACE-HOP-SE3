\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method{}, a general framework for pose distributions that are
exactly normalized by construction: a tree-structured softmax over any strict
hierarchical equivolumetric partition of a pose group yields a non-parametric
distribution whose log-likelihood at any pose is read off in closed form from a
single root-to-leaf path, with no retrieval grid and no sampled partition
function. We instantiated it on $\SOthree$ via the Hopf--HEALPix tree and on
$\SEthree$ via the Cartesian product of that tree with a bounded equivolumetric
translation octree; a single light cross-attention head on a vision transformer
realizes both, trained by single-ground-truth maximum likelihood with an $O(bL)$
beam-search MAP decoder.

On SYMSOL~I a single joint model attains $7.71$ average log-likelihood, above the
strongest RGB-only baseline ($7.33$, trained per shape), while evaluating any
rotation's exact likelihood in seven node scorings instead of scoring a
million-cell grid. The construction is strongest exactly where normalization is
usually most expensive---it comes within about a nat of the discretization ceiling on
the continuous shapes (cone, cylinder) and resolves the low-order discrete
symmetries well---and it is weakest on the high-order icosahedral object, whose
sixty equivalent modes must receive mass simultaneously from single-rotation
supervision. The efficiency and the remaining gap share one root cause: an
exactly normalized tree makes every path cheap but asks the model to distribute
probability consistently across many independent paths, which is the hard part of
high-order ambiguity.

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.}
Three limitations bound the present results. (i)~Covering many well-separated modes
requires splitting probability mass consistently across independent root-to-leaf
paths; this is where single-ground-truth maximum likelihood hurts most, and it is the
direct cause of the weaker icosahedral numbers. (ii)~The piecewise-constant density
caps the attainable log-likelihood at the discretization ceiling of
\cref{tab:ceiling}, so headroom on the discrete shapes is a resolution limit as
much as a modeling one. (iii)~The $\SEthree$ instantiation of \cref{sec:se3} is
fully constructed and numerically verified---its uniform predictor scores the
exact $-\ln(\pi^2 V_{\mathrm{box}})$ floor and its tree likelihood matches the
flat per-cell categorical to machine precision---but its evaluation on the
real-image benchmarks T-LESS and YCB-Video (\cref{sec:se3results}), together with
the SYMSOL~II marked-shape evaluation, is the current frontier rather than a
reported result. Carrying the verified $\SEthree$ construction through training
and evaluation on T-LESS and YCB-Video under the BOP protocol is the main
direction we are pursuing.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The construction admits an exact self-test: a uniform (zero-initialized) predictor
scores average log-likelihood $-\ln\Vso\approx-2.29$ on $\SOthree$ at \emph{every}
level---and, for the $\SEthree$ instance, $-\ln(\pi^2 V_{\mathrm{box}})$---which we
verify numerically before training. The grid is the \textsc{nested} Hopf--HEALPix
partition with $N(L)=72\cdot 8^{L}$ cells (\cref{sec:grid}), fully determined by
$L$; evaluation uses the published SYMSOL \texttt{tfds} release. Code and the
preprocessing pipeline will be released on acceptance.
