\begin{abstract}
Estimating the full distribution of an object's pose---rather than a single
estimate---is essential when symmetry, self-occlusion, or sensor noise make the
pose ambiguous. The dominant non-parametric approach, Implicit-PDF, represents
the distribution implicitly as an energy over $\SOthree$ and must evaluate a
dense equivolumetric grid of millions of rotations---both to approximate the
normalizer during training and to recover a normalized density at inference.
A hierarchical variant, SpyroPose, keeps the energy formulation but estimates
the partition function with importance sampling during training and evaluates a
sparse coarse-to-fine tree at inference, and extends to $\SEthree$ with an energy
pyramid. In both cases the distribution is an energy whose cost is tied to how many
poses are scored.

We revisit the problem from the opposite direction, and in general. For any pose
group with a \emph{strict} hierarchical equivolumetric partition---one in which
every cell splits into a fixed number of disjoint equal-volume children---a
distribution over the leaves is a tree-structured softmax: a categorical over the
coarsest cells and, at every expanded node, a categorical over its children. This
is an \emph{exactly} normalized probability by design, independent of the group: no
retrieval grid and no sampled partition function are ever needed, the log-likelihood
of any pose is read off in closed form from the $L{+}1$ nodes on its path (cost
$O(L)$ rather than $O(N)$ for $N$ cells), and a uniform predictor provably scores
$-\ln V_G$ at every resolution---a data-free self-test of the whole likelihood. We
instantiate the framework on $\SOthree$ via the Hopf--HEALPix grid (eight children
per node) and on $\SEthree$ via the Cartesian product of that rotation tree with a
bounded equivolumetric translation octree (sixty-four children per node). A
lightweight cross-attention head on an ImageNet-pretrained vision transformer
realizes the construction, zero-initialized so training starts exactly at the
uniform distribution, trained by maximum likelihood of the single ground-truth pose.

On SYMSOL~I a single joint model reaches $7.71$ average log-likelihood, surpassing
the strongest RGB-only baseline while reading any rotation's exact likelihood from a
seven-node path instead of a million-cell grid, with near-grid-floor beam-MAP
accuracy except on the high-order icosahedral object, whose sixty equivalent modes
are the hardest to resolve. The $\SEthree$ instance is exactly normalized by the
same construction---its uniform predictor provably scores $-\ln(\pi^2 V_{\mathrm{box}})$,
which we verify numerically to machine precision---and we set up its evaluation,
together with SYMSOL~II and the real-image BOP datasets T-LESS and YCB-Video, under
one protocol spanning both pose groups. Code and the preprocessing pipeline will be
released.
\end{abstract}
