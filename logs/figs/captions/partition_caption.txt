  \caption{\textbf{Equivolumetric partition of $\mathrm{SO}(3)$.} \emph{Top:} our Hopf--HEALPix base

  cell-volume ratio grows without bound with $\theta$-resolution ($3.7\times\!\to\!7.6\times\!\to\!15.3\times$),
  so a uniform categorical over its cells carries a $+0.11$--$0.14$\,nat log-likelihood bias relative to the true
  uniform density; our equal-volume tree has bias $0$. For scale, the SYMSOL-I state-of-the-art margin is
  $\approx\!0.4$\,nat.}
