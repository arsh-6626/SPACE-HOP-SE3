  \caption{\textbf{The Hopf--HEALPix discretization of $SO(3)$.} A single construction chain. The Hopf map
  $h:S^3\to S^2$ factors a rotation (Haar volume $\pi^2$) into a base point on $S^2$ and a roll angle on the
  $S^1$ fibre. The base sphere is partitioned by \textbf{HEALPix} into $N_{\mathrm{pix}}=12N_{\mathrm{side}}^2$
  \emph{equal-area} pixels and the roll into $N_\psi=6N_{\mathrm{side}}$ uniform bins; their product gives
  \textbf{equivolumetric} cells of measure $\mu(\mathrm{cell})=\pi^2/N(\ell)$, indexed by
  $c=\mathrm{pix}\cdot n_{\mathrm{roll}}+\mathrm{roll}$. Doubling $N_{\mathrm{side}}$ splits each pixel into $4$
  (HEALPix NESTED) and each roll bin into $2$, an $\times8$ equivolumetric refinement giving the hierarchical tree
  with $N(\ell)=72\cdot 8^{\ell}$ leaves. Panels are shaded $S^2$ globes in the decode-tree style, except the Hopf
  step, which shows the \emph{actual} Hopf fibres stereographically projected from $S^3$ to $\mathbb{R}^3$ (coloured
  by roll $\psi$, chosen roll in gold); the followed cell is gold throughout.}
  \label{fig:hopf_chain}
\end{figure*}
