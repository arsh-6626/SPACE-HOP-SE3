  \caption{\textbf{NeST: a tree-structured softmax over an equivolumetric partition is an exactly-normalized
  density.} An image $x$ is encoded to patch tokens that condition a categorical at every node of the
  Hopf--HEALPix partition tree: a $72$-way root softmax and an $8$-way softmax at each refinement. The density of a
  rotation is the product of the softmaxes along its root-to-leaf path times $N/V$. Because every node's children
  are equal Haar-volume, $\int p(R\mid x)\,\mathrm{d}R=1$ for \emph{any} network outputs (exact normalization), the
  uniform is recovered exactly ($-\ln\pi^2$), and the likelihood of any pose is read off by scoring only $L{+}1$
  nodes ($O(L)$) rather than a full grid.}
